About Us

RENDUS Technologies was founded in 2016 as a Software Development and Training company and has since grown rapidly to meet the enormous demand for next-generation technologies.

At RENDUS, we believe that every project should be successful. We leverage the best resources to offer innovative, high-quality training in technical, application, and professional development. We are committed to better scheduling, quality, service, and value for every client.

Most completed RENDUS Technologies instructor-led courses carry our Learning Guarantee. This allows students to repeat most instructor-led courses (same version) free of charge within six months of course completion.

At RENDUS Technologies, every class is intentionally small, giving each student direct one-on-one access to the instructor. In every course, students begin applying skills immediately through practical exercises while instructors provide continuous feedback.

Our Aim

Our vision is to continuously update people's capabilities to align with the technologies of today and tomorrow. We deliver best-in-class training and development that empower learners through pre-built workflow packages and a modern management platform (GAIN).

We follow an agile, phased implementation approach that enables faster ROI while minimizing risk, supported by an active community of clients, partners, and experts.

Whether organizations realize it or not, skills are often the key limiter to success. Technology and process are essential for achieving objectives, but the larger challenge is closing skill gaps that reduce technology ROI. Organizations that use technology to accelerate growth are also those that transform their people's capabilities to maximize investments.

Director's Note

Our instructors are software experts first and trainers second. They bring real project experience and help teams deliver more successful outcomes. Our technical staff has played a key role in improving software development practices across numerous organizations in India.

Team RENDUS provides an immersive learning environment away from daily workplace distractions, helping maximize learning retention. Depending on the model that works best for each learner, we offer flexible options ranging from custom courses and micro-learning to live online sessions.

At RENDUS Technologies, we provide training that suits all learning styles and career stages.
